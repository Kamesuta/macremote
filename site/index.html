Control Your PC

See and control your Mac from your phone. It reconnects by itself when you come back to the app, and it is reachable only over your own private network.

What you get

  • Pinch to zoom and two-finger pan
  • Trackpad mode: move, tap, right-click, drag and scroll
  • A text box to type or paste whole commands to your Mac
  • Nothing stored on our servers, because we have none

How it works

You run a small program on your Mac. It uses the Screen Sharing built into macOS and a free Tailscale account to connect your phone to your Mac, directly and privately.

Download for Mac (free). Unzip it, open Terminal in the folder, and run ./install.sh.

Windows support is planned.
