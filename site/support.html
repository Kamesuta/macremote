Support

Setup takes about five minutes.

Set up your Mac

  1. Turn on Screen Sharing: System Settings → General → Sharing → Screen Sharing.
  2. Install Node 20+ and Tailscale, and sign in to Tailscale on both your Mac and your phone.
  3. Download the Mac program and unzip it. In Terminal, go to the folder and run ./install.sh.
  4. Open the address it prints, in the app or in Safari, and sign in.

Troubleshooting

  • The page won't load. The server may not be running. On the Mac, run launchctl list | grep macremote, or run ./install.sh again.
  • It loads but the screen won't connect. Screen Sharing may be off, or the Mac username or password is wrong.
  • The address won't open on your phone. Tailscale must be connected on both devices.
  • It shows Reconnecting for a long time. Fully close the app and reopen it. If it keeps happening, check that your Mac isn't asleep.
